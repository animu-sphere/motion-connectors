# Code of Conduct

This project aims to be a welcoming place to work on live motion input for OpenUSD and the tooling around it. Everyone who
participates in the repository, its issues, pull requests or related project
spaces is expected to help keep it that way.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient and specific, especially when correcting an idea.
- Assume good intent while still discussing the technical impact of a change.
- Welcome questions from people at different levels of familiarity.
- Focus criticism on the work, not on a person.
- Respect the licenses and privacy of the people whose motion is captured, of asset creators, and of
  other contributors.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, threats, sexualized conduct,
doxxing, spam, deliberate disruption and sharing someone else's private
information are not welcome. Deliberately ignoring the project's license and
security boundaries is also unacceptable.

## Reporting a concern

For a public disagreement, keep the discussion focused on the technical work.
For a private or sensitive concern, follow the private reporting guidance in
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) and say that the report concerns community conduct.
Please do not include personal information in a public issue.

Maintainers will review reports as promptly and privately as practical, and will
give people a fair opportunity to explain what happened.
